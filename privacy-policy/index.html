Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy describes privacy practices for myelinatedmelanin.com, operated by Karista Williams under the name Myelinated Melanin.

Visiting the site

You can browse this site without creating an account or entering payment information. When your browser requests a page, hosting and network providers may process technical details such as your IP address, browser type, and requested page to deliver and protect the site.

TikTok content

Opening the TikTok section can load content directly from TikTok. When that happens, your browser connects to TikTok. TikTok may receive device and browser information and use cookies or similar technologies under its Privacy Policy.

If we enable the planned TikTok API connection, we will use it to read the authorized Myelinated Melanin creator profile and its public posts for display here. Visitors can browse without signing in to TikTok through this site.

Links to other services

This site links to Amazon, Whop, Teachers Pay Teachers, and the Digital Learning Wall. Those services handle any information you provide to them, including account, enrollment, or payment details, under their own privacy policies.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about information you share with us, contact Myelinated Melanin through its official TikTok profile. If you send us a message, we may use the information in your message to respond.
